<p class="isSelectedEnd">Panchmukhi Hanuman means "Five-Faced Hanuman." According to Hindu scriptures, Lord Hanuman assumed this powerful form during a battle to defeat evil forces and protect righteousness.</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">The five faces of Panchmukhi Hanuman represent:</p>
<ul data-spread="false">
<li>Hanuman Face &ndash; Courage, strength, and devotion.</li>
<li>Narasimha Face &ndash; Protection from fear and negative energies.</li>
<li>Garuda Face &ndash; Protection from harmful influences and obstacles.</li>
<li>Varaha Face &ndash; Prosperity, stability, and success.</li>
<li>Hayagriva Face &ndash; Knowledge, wisdom, and intelligence.</li>
</ul>
<p class="isSelectedEnd">Together, these five divine forms create a powerful symbol of spiritual protection and positive energy.</p>
